## cp_violation

### ckm_cp_phase

1. $K_{ij}=\Lambda \int d^3x\,d^3y\,K(x,y)\psi_i^*(x)\psi_j(y)$ and $\delta_{\text{CKM}}=\arg(K_{ij})$ (analytic closed form used: $K_{ij}=\Lambda \frac{4\pi}{3}\ell^3[1+(\ell/\sigma_{\rm eff})^2]^{-3/2}e^{i(\phi_0+\phi_1\ell_{\rm eff})}$)

### jarlskog_ckm

1. $J_{\text{CKM}} = \text{Im}(V_{ud} V_{cs} V_{us}^* V_{cd}^*)$ where $V$ is CKM matrix with CP phase $\delta_{\text{CKM}}$

### pmns_cp_phase

1. $K_{ij}=\Lambda \int d^3x\,d^3y\,K(x,y)\psi_i^*(x)\psi_j(y)$ and $\delta_{\text{PMNS}}=\arg(K_{ij})$ (analytic closed form used: $K_{ij}=\Lambda \frac{4\pi}{3}\ell^3[1+(\ell/\sigma_{\rm eff})^2]^{-3/2}e^{i(\phi_0+\phi_1\ell_{\rm eff})}$)

### jarlskog_pmns

1. $J_{\text{PMNS}} = \text{Im}(U_{e1} U_{\mu 2} U_{e2}^* U_{\mu 1}^*)$ where $U$ is PMNS matrix with CP phase $\delta_{\text{PMNS}}$
